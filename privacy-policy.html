📅 Privacy Policy - Kalendara Sadaa

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Kalendara Sadaa ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

  • Ad Interaction Data: Data related to ad views and clicks (via Google AdMob).

2. How We Use Information

  • To display relevant advertisements

We use Google AdMob for advertising. Google may collect data for personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Storage

All reminders and notes are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or store your data on external servers.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: Used to display advertisements

6. Your Rights

You can change notification preferences in your device settings. You can also opt out of personalized ads in your Google account settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
