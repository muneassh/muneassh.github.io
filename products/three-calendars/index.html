About the App

Three Calendars is the ultimate calendar app that brings together Ethiopian (Amharic), Gregorian (English), and Hijri (Arabic) calendars in one beautiful interface. Perfect for anyone who needs to track dates across multiple calendar systems.

  • Three Calendar Systems Ethiopian (Amharic), Gregorian (English), and Hijri (Arabic) calendars with full month names and special events.
  • Smart Reminders Create reminders that work across all three calendar systems. Never miss important dates again.
  • Home Screen Widget Quick view of all three calendar dates directly from your home screen without opening the app.
  • Circular Clock Interface Beautiful and intuitive circular interface for easy date selection and navigation.
  • Special Events Built-in Ethiopian and Hijri special events with recommendations and notifications.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is today's Ethiopian date?
The app automatically shows today's Ethiopian date in Amharic, including the month (Meskerem to Pagume) and year. You can view it instantly when opening the app.
How to convert Gregorian to Hijri date?
Simply select any date in Gregorian calendar and the app will instantly show the equivalent Hijri date. The circular clock interface makes it easy to navigate through dates.
Do reminders work across all calendars?
Yes! When you create a reminder, it automatically works across all three calendar systems. You'll receive notifications regardless of which calendar you're viewing.
Is the app free?
Yes, the app is completely free to download and use. It includes ads to support continued development, but all features are available without payment.
Does it work offline?
Yes! The app works completely offline. All data, including reminders and calendar calculations, are stored locally on your device.
