👨‍💻 About Muneassh Apps

Muneassh Apps is a small independent app development studio based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We specialize in creating high-quality calendar and productivity apps for Android.

📱 Our Apps

Ethiopian GC & Hijri Calendar

✅ Live on Play Store

Three calendars in one app: Ethiopian, Gregorian & Hijri with reminders and notes.

Kalendara Sadaa

🔬 In Review / Testing

Full Afan Oromo version. Kalendara Itoophiyaa, Giregoriyaan & Hijrii fi yaadannoo.

🎯 Mission

We aim to make calendar apps accessible to everyone, in every language. Our apps are designed to be simple, beautiful, and useful for daily life.

🌍 Languages

Our apps support multiple languages to serve users worldwide:

  • English - For international users
  • አማርኛ (Amharic) - For Ethiopian users
  • Afaan Oromo - For Oromo-speaking users
  • العربية (Arabic) - For Arabic-speaking users
